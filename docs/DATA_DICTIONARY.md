# Data Dictionary (Mart Layer)

This document describes the key metrics and columns available in the final Gold/Mart tables.

## 1. mart_sales_overview
Tracks overall business health on a monthly basis.
- `sales_month`: Start date of the month.
- `total_orders`: Number of unique delivered orders.
- `total_revenue`: Sum of all payment values for delivered orders.
- `average_order_value`: `total_revenue` / `total_orders`.
- `unique_customers`: Number of distinct customers who purchased.
- `avg_monthly_review_score`: Average review score for the month.

## 2. mart_delivery_performance
Tracks SLA and logistics efficiency.
- `delivery_month`: Start date of the month (based on purchase).
- `total_delivered_orders`: Total orders successfully delivered.
- `avg_delivery_days`: Average days between purchase and customer delivery.
- `late_deliveries_count`: Number of orders delivered after estimated date.
- `late_delivery_rate`: Percentage of orders delivered late.

## 3. mart_product_analytics
Insights into product performance.
- `product_category_name`: English name of the category.
- `unique_products_sold`: Number of distinct products sold in the category.
- `total_category_orders`: Number of times products in this category were ordered.
- `total_category_revenue`: Total revenue generated by the category.
- `avg_category_review_score`: Average review score for products in this category.

## 4. mart_customer_rfm
Customer segmentation based on RFM model.
- `customer_unique_id`: Unique identifier for a customer.
- `recency_days`: Days since last purchase.
- `frequency`: Total number of orders.
- `monetary`: Total amount spent.
- `r_score`: Recency score (1-5, 5 is best/most recent).
- `f_score`: Frequency score (1-5, 5 is best/most frequent).
- `m_score`: Monetary score (1-5, 5 is best/highest spender).

## 5. mart_seller_ranking
Seller performance evaluation.
- `seller_id`: Unique identifier for the seller.
- `total_orders`: Total orders fulfilled.
- `total_revenue`: Total revenue generated.
- `avg_review_score`: Average rating received.
- `revenue_rank`: Rank based on total revenue.
- `review_rank`: Rank based on review score (minimum 10 orders).

## 6. mart_geo_analysis
Spatial distribution of the business.
- `customer_state`: State of the customer.
- `customer_city`: City of the customer.
- `total_orders`: Number of orders in this region.
- `total_revenue`: Total revenue from this region.
- `avg_delivery_days`: Average delivery time to this region.
